# Last Stand community rules

This project should be enjoyable to build and play. These rules apply to issues,
pull requests, reviews and other project spaces, regardless of experience level
or whether a contribution was assisted by AI.

- Be respectful. Critique ideas and code with specific reasons; do not attack
  people. Welcome questions and give others room to learn.
- No harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, impersonation
  or sharing another person's private information.
- Keep discussion relevant. No spam, scams, repeated demands for attention or
  coordinated pile-ons. Do not post credentials or private game/save files.
- Describe your work and evidence honestly. Give credit and do not present
  someone else's work as your own.
- Respect review decisions and other contributors' time. Disagreement is fine;
  state the concern calmly and let maintainers resolve scope or ownership.

Maintainers may request edits, remove harmful content, lock discussions or
restrict participation when these rules are violated. For a disputed technical
decision, explain the specific concern in the existing discussion without
personal attacks; a maintainer will decide how the project proceeds.

To report abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's
[content-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not repost private information or harassment as evidence in a public issue.
There is currently no project-specific private reporting address; do not assume
an issue is confidential. GitHub handles reports under its own policies; this
project has no separate private support channel.
